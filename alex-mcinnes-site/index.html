A Scottish rider raised in the UAE

Alex comes from the north of Scotland, a part of the world that almost never produces a professional rally-raid rider. The odds of getting from there to the Dakar are tiny: very few Scottish athletes have ever taken part in the race, let alone finished it.

He has lived in Dubai since 2006, when he was a young child. Growing up with the desert on his doorstep and the UAE’s sporting system behind him shaped him into the athlete he is, and in January 2027 he aims to become the first Scottish rider to finish the Dakar in Saudi Arabia.

Raised in the UAE

The UAE is where Alex learned to race, came through the national championship and won the title in 2025. He carries that pride to every start line.

A safe choice for UAE brands

Based in Dubai and available for appearances across the Emirates. UAE partners such as MotoZone, his race team, and Dawlati already back him. Professional and respectful on and off the bike, he races where UAE companies do business: the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the wider GCC.

Giving back

The UAE gave Alex his start, and he wants to give back: with his partners, opening the sport to young riders here through rider days, school visits and talks on coming back from injury, and carrying the UAE’s story to the Dakar start line.

Why partner with Alex in 2026

Regional results, global potential

Alex is a UAE-grown talent with a record across the GCC and on international stages. As the 2025 UAE and Qatar national champion, he connects Middle Eastern roots with world-level competition.

Back from a serious injury

In late 2025 Alex came through a career-threatening L2 and L5 vertebral injury. His comeback is a story of grit and determination that gives brands an authentic connection to perseverance.

The 2026–2027 mission

Win the FIM Bajas World Cup title, then make history as the first Scottish rider to finish the Dakar Rally in Saudi Arabia.
